US · Mega scale

Emma Chamberlain

Lifestyle ritual → replenishable consumable
What's actually being sold

Coffee and matcha. Specifically, the consumable version of a daily ritual she already performs on camera. The product is the habit, packaged.

Why it works

The audience follows her. The product is a way to extend the companion relationship — by participating in the same morning ritual at home.

She's not selling coffee. She's selling the version of Tuesday morning where she makes the coffee.

What to copy

The habit → consumable → repeat pattern. If your content shows a daily ritual, that ritual is the product.

What NOT to copy

Opening a coffee company. Emma's supply chain took years and capital. Most creators should sell the ritual digitally or via preorder first.
